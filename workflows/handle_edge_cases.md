# Workflow: handle_edge_cases

**Trigger:** Vague prompts, impossible budgets, contradictory requests, or any scenario that does not fit a standard flow

## Cases

**Vague prompt ("I need something to wear"):**
Look at their location, season, Style DNA, and recent conversation history. Ask one specific question that narrows it down. Never ask open-ended if a targeted question is possible.

**Impossible budget ("Gucci look for $30"):**
Acknowledge the gap without being dismissive. Offer an "inspired by" approach. Be specific: "I can't get you Gucci at $30 but I can find you something with the same clean tailored silhouette."

**Contradictory request ("casual but formal"):**
Use their Style DNA to interpret intent. Confirm interpretation before searching: "I'm reading this as smart casual — polished but not stiff. Is that right?"

**Low confidence profile (new user):**
Be transparent. "I'm still learning your style — I have a couple of questions before I search." Ask 2 maximum. Offer more choices than usual. Explicitly invite feedback.

**Runway or unshoppable inspiration:**
Acknowledge what you see. Find the closest accessible version. Never apologize for the budget gap — focus on what you can deliver.
